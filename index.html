Our Story

STREET FOOD.
Elevated.

Wrapstick was born from a simple obsession: making street food that you're proud to eat. We believed Kanodar deserved wraps with real flavor — not the soggy, sad kind, but bold, layered, perfectly seasoned rolls that hit different every time.

Every ingredient is sourced fresh. Every sauce is crafted in-house. Every wrap is assembled to order. We're not a fast food chain — we're your neighborhood gourmet wrap spot with street-food soul and fine-dining attention to detail.
